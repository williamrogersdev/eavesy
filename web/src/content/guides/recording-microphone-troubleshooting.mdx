First listen to the saved audio. Missing text does not always mean missing microphone sound: capture can succeed while recognition is unavailable, inaccurate, or still processing. Diagnose the recording and its transcript separately.

## Check the physical input

Open macOS Sound settings and choose the microphone you expect to use. Speak and check the input level. Confirm the device is not muted and that a headset or external microphone has not disconnected. Bluetooth and USB device changes can alter routing.

If macOS itself receives no signal, fix that before restarting a long Eavesy session. Move closer to the microphone and reduce competing audio for the short test.

## Check permissions

In **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, review Microphone access for Eavesy. Meeting and Video can also need Screen Recording or system-audio access. On-device captions use Speech Recognition. Automatic pasting uses Accessibility, which is a different permission from audio capture.

After changing permissions, follow any macOS restart prompt and reopen Eavesy if needed. Permission names can differ by OS version; check the current [Apple privacy guidance](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/control-access-to-your-microphone-on-mac-mchla1b1e1fe/mac) if you cannot find the control.

## Make a small recording

Use **Talk** to isolate the microphone. Say a unique sentence, stop after ten seconds, and play back the audio from that capture. If it is audible, repeat in Meeting or Video, the mode used when the failure occurred. Add computer sound only after microphone capture works on its own.

A moving recording timer proves that a session started, not that usable sound reached the saved file. Playback is the useful check.

## If the sound is there but text is missing

Check whether the capture is still processing, whether you are signed in for cloud transcription, and whether on-device recognition is available and permitted. Review the spoken-language setting. Do not delete the original audio while investigating.

For support, record the Eavesy version, macOS version, input device, capture mode, whether playback has your voice, and any visible error. Avoid sending a private meeting merely to demonstrate the problem; a short synthetic test is usually enough.
