Meeting mode records your microphone and the audio your Mac plays, so you get
both sides of a call. It does not record your screen. For that, use
[Video](/docs/video).

Nothing joins the call and there is no meeting link to share. It works the same
with [Zoom](/integrations/zoom), [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet),
[Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams), [Webex](/integrations/webex),
[Slack huddles](/integrations/slack) and
[in-person conversations](/integrations/in-person).

Can't record from your Mac? Send the [Notetaker](/docs/notetaker) to a Google
Meet or Teams call instead. It joins as a participant.

<Callout type="note">
Tell people when you record them, and follow your organization's policy and
the consent rules where you are.
</Callout>

## Record a meeting

1. Open the launcher with **⌘⇧Space** and choose **Meeting**.
2. Talk as usual. The bar shows how long you've been recording, and you can
   open it to see live captions.
3. Choose stop when you're done.

Hearing the other side of a call needs the Screen Recording permission.

## Live captions

Live captions are made on your Mac as people speak, and are on by default. Each
line is labelled by where it came from: your microphone, or the call's audio.
Turn them off under **Settings → Live captions**. They need the Speech
Recognition permission.

Eavesy does not tell individual speakers apart. Everyone on the call's side
shares one label.

## After you stop

Without an account, the audio is saved in a folder named like
`2026-06-15 Meeting recording`, next to a `notes.md` you can write in.

When you're signed in, audio uploads in segments while you record, so a long
meeting is not lost if your Mac restarts or the network drops. After you stop:

- a cloud transcript replaces the live captions, with lines marked **You** and
  **Them**, and
- a brief is written on top of it: a title, a short summary, decisions and
  action items.

The brief and transcript are written into the same folder as `summary.md` and
`transcript.md`. The folder keeps its original name even after the brief
suggests a better title.

## Getting clean audio

- Eavesy records what your Mac is playing. If the call plays through a device
  you've muted, it won't be heard.
- Headphones are fine. The call's audio is recorded before it reaches them.
- In a room, the built-in microphone is usually enough. Make a short test
  recording before anything important.
