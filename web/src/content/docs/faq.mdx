## Does Eavesy join my calls as a bot?

No. Eavesy captures the audio and screen on your Mac, so nothing appears in the
participant list. Tell participants when required and follow your organization&rsquo;s
policy and applicable consent laws. It works with any calling app—and in person.
[How capture works →](/docs/capturing-screen-and-audio)

## Do I need an account to try it?

Yes. The [browser recorder](/docs/browser-recorder) uses a free account so the
finished transcript and brief have a private place to process and remain available.
The Mac app pairs to that same account.

## Which apps does it support?

Anything that plays audio or shows on your screen —
[Zoom](/integrations/zoom), [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet),
[Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams), [Webex](/integrations/webex),
[Slack huddles](/integrations/slack), and
[in-person conversations](/integrations/in-person). No extension or admin
approval required. [See all integrations →](/docs/integrations-overview)

## What languages are supported?

Transcription handles dozens of languages automatically — you don&rsquo;t need
to set one. [Transcripts &amp; summaries →](/docs/transcripts-and-summaries)

## Do you train AI models on my conversations?

Eavesy does not train models on your recordings. Cloudflare Workers AI and
Anthropic&rsquo;s commercial API state that customer content is not used for model
training unless the customer explicitly opts in. A connected OpenRouter account
follows the privacy settings you control there.
[Privacy &amp; your data →](/docs/privacy-and-data)

## Where are my files?

With the Mac app, every session is written to a folder on your disk as Markdown,
JSON, and audio — formats you (and your agents) own.
[The session folder format →](/docs/session-folder-format)

## Can my AI agent read my sessions?

Yes — that&rsquo;s the point. Paste a brief into any agent, or point an agent
with filesystem access at your Eavesy folder.
[Context for AI agents →](/docs/context-for-agents)

## What does it cost?

Eavesy is free to use. Everyone gets the same daily fair-use limits.
[Fair-use limits →](/docs/fair-use-limits)
