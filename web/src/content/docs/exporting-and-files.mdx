With the Mac app, every session is written to a **real folder on your disk** —
no lock-in, in formats any tool (or agent) can read.

## The session folder

Each session becomes a folder containing:

- `summary.md` — the structured brief in Markdown
- `transcript.md` — the full transcript
- `meeting.json` — metadata (participants, timestamps, action items)
- the **audio** file

[See the full folder format →](/docs/session-folder-format)

Point Eavesy&rsquo;s save location at your [Obsidian](/integrations/obsidian)
vault and sessions show up as notes you can link and tag — or point an agent
straight at the folder.

## Exporting

- **A single session** — export to Markdown, JSON, audio, or a ZIP that mirrors
  the folder layout.
- **Everything** — Settings → Privacy → **Export everything** produces a full
  archive of your account.

<Callout type="privacy">
Your local folders are yours and stay on your disk. Deleting your account purges
the cloud copies on a clear schedule but never touches your local files. See
[Privacy &amp; your data](/docs/privacy-and-data).
</Callout>
