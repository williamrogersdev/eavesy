Eavesy doesn&rsquo;t plug into your tools with bots or APIs — it captures the
audio and screen on your Mac, so it works with **anything that makes sound or
shows on your display**. That makes &ldquo;integrations&rdquo; mostly a matter of
where the audio comes from and where the brief goes.

## Video calls — no bot, no admin

Record any call by capturing its system audio. Nothing joins the meeting and
there&rsquo;s nothing for IT to approve:

- [Zoom](/integrations/zoom)
- [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet)
- [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams)
- [Webex](/integrations/webex)
- [Slack huddles](/integrations/slack)

<Callout type="note">
Because capture happens at the OS level, these work whatever account the calling
app uses — no browser extension or Workspace admin approval needed.
</Callout>

## Notes &amp; knowledge — where the brief lands

Send each session&rsquo;s brief into the tools you already think in:

- [Obsidian](/integrations/obsidian) — point Eavesy&rsquo;s save folder at your
  vault and sessions become linkable notes.
- [Notion](/integrations/notion) — paste or import the Markdown brief.
- [Apple Notes](/integrations/apple-notes) — copy a clean brief straight in.

## In person

No meeting link required — [record an in-person conversation](/integrations/in-person)
with your Mac&rsquo;s microphone and get the same transcript and brief.
