Not everything an agent needs is a recording. The launcher also keeps
screenshots, clipboard text and images, notes, and files.

On the Mac, these are files on your disk. When you are signed in, notes
(including clipboard text and dropped Markdown or text files), screenshots and
pasted images are also uploaded to your account so they appear on your other
signed-in Macs. Screenshots and images show in the web app too; notes don't
yet. Signed out, they all stay on this Mac. Imported audio and video files are
never uploaded. None of them are available over MCP.

## Screenshots

Open the launcher with **⌘⇧Space** and choose **Screenshot**, then pick an
area of your screen. Screenshots need macOS 14 or later and the Screen
Recording permission.

Screenshots you take with macOS itself can be added too. Turn on
**Settings → Screenshots → Add macOS screenshots automatically**.

## Clipboard

Copy something, open the launcher, and press **⌘V**. Text is saved as a note
and an image is saved as a PNG.

## Notes

Press **N** in the launcher to jump to the composer and write a quick note. The
composer also takes pasted text and dropped files.

## Import files

Drop a file onto the launcher to keep a copy of it:

- images (PNG, JPEG, HEIC, GIF, WebP, TIFF, BMP) are kept as images,
- Markdown and plain text files become notes, up to 1 MB, and
- audio (M4A, MP3, WAV, AAC, AIFF, CAF, FLAC) and video (MP4, MOV, M4V) files
  are kept as they are.

The capture takes the file's name as its title.

Folders can't be dropped. Drop the files inside instead.

## What gets saved

Each item gets its own folder in your recordings folder, with one file and a
`capture.json`: `note.md` for notes, `image.png` for images, or
`screenshot.png` for screenshots. Imported files keep their extension, for
example `image.jpg` or `audio.mp3`. See [your files](/docs/session-folder-format) for
names and layout.

## On the web

The web app has its own capture bar for screenshots, screen and webcam video,
and audio. Those are uploaded to your account. See
[Eavesy on the web](/docs/browser-recorder).
