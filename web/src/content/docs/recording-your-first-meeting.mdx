This walks through a complete recording, from pressing record to walking away
with a finished brief you can hand to your AI.

## 1. Start the recording

With the Mac app, launch a recording from the menu bar or with your global
hotkey — no need to switch apps first. In the browser, click **record** on the
dashboard and grant microphone or screen-sharing access when prompted.

<Callout type="note">
Because the Mac app captures your screen and system audio, there&rsquo;s no bot to
invite or participant-list entry. Tell people when required and follow your
organization&rsquo;s policy and applicable consent laws.
</Callout>

## 2. Watch it work

The recording bar shows elapsed time and whether capture is active. During long
Mac sessions, sealed audio segments upload and can add processed transcript and
notes in batches. The browser recorder uploads the completed capture after you
stop; it does not show live captions.

## 3. Stop and review

Press **stop**. After upload and processing, a final pass produces the polished brief: title, TL;DR,
decisions, and owner-tagged follow-ups, all sitting on top of the full
transcript.

## 4. Keep, paste, or share it

- **Paste it into your AI** — copy the brief straight into Claude, Cursor, or
  ChatGPT. [How that works →](/docs/context-for-agents)
- **Export it** — Markdown, JSON, audio, or a ZIP.
  [Exporting &amp; your files →](/docs/exporting-and-files)
- **Find it on disk** — with the Mac app it&rsquo;s already written to your
  folder as Markdown, JSON, and audio.

<Callout type="tip">
Want a different structure? Open the completed recording, choose a built-in
format or one of your [custom templates](/docs/templates), and select
**Regenerate**. You can also edit the Markdown directly.
</Callout>
