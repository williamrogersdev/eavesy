Every recording produces two linked artifacts: the **transcript** (the complete
record of what was said) and the **summary** (a structured distillation on top
of it).

## The transcript

- Mac recordings are sealed into **recoverable rolling segments** and processed
  as those segments upload.
- Browser recordings upload and process **after you stop**.
- Supports **dozens of languages** automatically.
- Copyable and **exportable as Markdown**.

The transcript is always included on every plan. It&rsquo;s never paywalled
behind a summary-only tier.

<Callout type="note" title="Rolling is not instant">
The Mac app processes durable audio segments during long recordings, so text can
arrive in batches. The browser recorder waits until capture is complete. Eavesy
does not currently provide sub-second live captions or speaker diarization.
</Callout>

## The summary

After you stop recording and processing finishes, Eavesy generates:

- a **title** and **TL;DR**,
- **decisions** made in the session,
- **action items** with owners and due dates where mentioned, and
- **open questions** to follow up on.

It&rsquo;s structured as clean Markdown, so you can copy the whole brief and
paste it straight into Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT — your agent gets the full
context in one shot. [More on feeding agents →](/docs/context-for-agents)

## Working with the output

Copy or export the Markdown brief and transcript from the completed session.
You can edit a brief directly, restore the generated version later, or
regenerate it with a built-in format or [custom template](/docs/templates).

<Callout type="tip">
Looking for an old decision across every session? Use
[semantic search](/docs/finding-past-sessions) to find a relevant excerpt and
open the matching session.
</Callout>
