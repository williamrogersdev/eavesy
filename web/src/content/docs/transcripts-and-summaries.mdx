Every recording can have two kinds of text: live captions made on your Mac
while you record, and a cloud transcript made after it uploads.

## Live captions

The Mac app writes captions on your Mac as you speak. They are on by default
and work without an account. They need the Speech Recognition permission, and
you can turn them off under **Settings → Live captions** in the Mac app.

Talk uses the same on-device recognition for the text it pastes.

## Cloud transcript

When you're signed in, recordings are also transcribed in the cloud. The cloud
transcript replaces the live captions once it's ready. It's the complete
record the brief is written from.

- On the Mac, audio uploads in segments while you record, so text can arrive
  in batches before you stop.
- In the browser, audio uploads in segments of about four minutes.

Meetings are recorded as two tracks, so lines are labelled **You** for your
microphone and **Them** for the call's audio. Eavesy does not tell individual
people apart within a track.

## Spoken language

Transcription is set to English by default. To change it, open the web app and
go to **Settings → Transcription → Spoken language**. Pick your language, or
choose to detect it automatically. A fixed language is more accurate when you
always speak the same one.

## Custom words

Add names, products and jargon under **Settings → Transcription → Custom
words**, one per line or separated by commas. You can add up to 100. They help
both cloud transcripts and on-device captions on a signed-in Mac spell those
words your way.
