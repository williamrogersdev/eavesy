## What stays on your Mac

Without an account, nothing leaves your Mac. Captures are files in your
recordings folder, and Talk text and live captions are made on-device.

Imported audio and video files stay on your Mac even when you're signed in.
Notes, including clipboard text and dropped Markdown or text files, upload
once you sign in (see below).

## What goes to the cloud

When you sign in, the Mac app uploads recording audio (Talk, Meeting and
Video) so it can be transcribed and summarized. Your account then holds the
audio, transcripts, briefs, notes and search index for those recordings.

A signed-in Mac also uploads its screenshots, pasted images and notes to your
account, so they appear in the history on your other signed-in Macs.
Screenshots and images also show in the web app. Sign out and new ones stay
on that Mac only. Delete a screenshot or image in the web app to remove the
cloud copy. Notes don't show in the web app yet, so their cloud copies go
when you delete your account.

The web app stores what you make there: browser recordings, capture bar
screenshots and videos, dictations and drops.

When you send the [Notetaker](/docs/notetaker) to a call, it records the call's
audio, the participants' display names and who was speaking when. It doesn't
record video. The notetaker runs on Cloudflare, and its recordings are stored
and deleted like your other recordings.

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with Cloudflare-managed
encryption.

## Who processes it

| Provider | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, storage and database, the Notetaker, plus Workers AI for transcription and search embeddings |
| Anthropic | Briefs, action items and chat |
| Clerk | Sign-in |
| OpenRouter | Only if you connect your own account |
| PostHog | Product analytics and scrubbed error reports, never recording content |

Eavesy does not train models on your content. Cloudflare and Anthropic state
that customer content is not used for training unless the customer opts in. If
you connect OpenRouter, the privacy settings you choose there apply.

## Your own AI keys

In the web app under **Settings → AI providers**, you can connect your
OpenRouter account or add an Anthropic API key. Summaries and chat then bill to
your account. Keys are entered on the web and stored encrypted. Requests still
pass through Eavesy so it can build the prompt.

The Mac app keeps only its device token in your Keychain. It never holds your
AI keys.

## Export

- **Everything:** in the web app, open **Settings → Your data** and choose
  **Export everything**.
- **One recording:** download its bundle from the recording's page.
- **On a Mac:** your files are already on disk.

Exports count toward the [fair-use limits](/docs/fair-use-limits).

## Delete

- **One recording:** delete it in the app. Its cloud copies, including audio,
  transcript and search index, are permanently removed within a day.
- **One note:** deleting it on a Mac removes it from that Mac only. Its cloud
  copy stays until you delete your account.
- **Your account:** open **Settings → Your data** and choose **Delete**.
  Everything in the cloud is removed and your Mac is signed out.

Deleting in the cloud never touches the files on your Mac.
