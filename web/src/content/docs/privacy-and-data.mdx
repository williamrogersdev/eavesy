Eavesy is built local-first and privacy-first. Here&rsquo;s exactly how your
data is handled.

## Where it lives

- **Mac app:** source files are written to a folder on your disk. A private cloud
  copy supports transcription, search, sync, and recovery after you pair the app.
- **Browser:** recordings, transcripts, and briefs are stored under your signed-in
  account so processing can finish and you can return to them later.

## Encryption

- **In transit:** TLS.
- **At rest:** Cloudflare-managed encryption protects audio in object storage
  and metadata in the database.

## Model-provider policies

Eavesy does not train models on your recordings. Cloudflare states that Workers
AI customer content is not used to train models or improve its services without
explicit consent. Anthropic states that its commercial API data is not used for
model training unless the customer explicitly opts in. If you connect your own
OpenRouter account, provider training and retention follow the privacy settings
you control in OpenRouter.

<Callout type="privacy">
Capture only happens while a recording is running, and only with the macOS
permissions you grant. Stop a recording and nothing is being captured.
</Callout>

## Your controls

- **Export everything** from Settings → Your data, or a single meeting bundle or
  capture from its detail page.
- **Delete** any recording or capture in the app; its cloud copies (audio,
  transcript, search index) are permanently removed within a day.
- **Delete your account** from Settings → Your data. Everything stored in the
  cloud is permanently removed and your Mac is signed out; local folders remain
  yours.

For the full list of subprocessors, see the [security page](/security) and the
