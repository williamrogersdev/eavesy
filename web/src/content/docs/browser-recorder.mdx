The browser recorder lets you try Eavesy without a download. A free account keeps
the finished transcript and brief private while they process and gives you a place
to return to them later.

## How it works

1. Go to [eavesy.com](/) and click **Try free in your browser**.
2. Grant microphone access when your browser prompts you.
3. Record for as long as you need. Audio uploads every few minutes while you
   record, so a long session is safe even if your connection drops.
4. Get a transcript and brief shortly after you stop, then copy it into your AI or export to
   Markdown and audio.

<Callout type="note" title="What it captures">
The browser recorder captures your **microphone** and can include shared-tab or
shared-screen audio when your browser supports it. It does not save the screen
video with the recording. For full-fidelity screen + system audio + microphone
capture, use the [Mac app](/docs/installing-the-mac-app).
</Callout>

## Privacy

Browser recordings belong to your signed-in account. Eavesy does not train
models on your content; platform-provider and connected-account policies are
explained in [Privacy &amp; your data](/docs/privacy-and-data), alongside storage,
export, and deletion details.

## Browser vs. Mac app

| | Browser | Mac app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account required | Free account | Yes |
| Captures | Mic + supported shared audio | Screen + system audio + mic |
| Session length | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Files written to disk | Export | Automatic |
| Library | Yes | Yes |

When you&rsquo;re ready for full sessions, [download the Mac app](/download/mac).
