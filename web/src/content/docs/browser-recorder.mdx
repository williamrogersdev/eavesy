The web app at eavesy.com works on any computer with a modern browser. It
needs a free account.

## Record in the browser

Open the web app and start a recording. Grant microphone access when your
browser asks. If you share a tab, its audio can be recorded too, where your
browser supports it.

Audio uploads in segments of about four minutes while you record, so a long
recording is safe if your connection drops. After you stop, you get a
transcript and a brief, the same as on the Mac.

<Callout type="note" title="Not saved as video">
The browser recorder keeps audio only. It does not save the shared screen as
video.
</Callout>

## The capture bar

The capture bar in the web app records audio from your microphone, video from
your webcam or screen, or a screenshot. You review each one before saving.
Everything from the capture bar is uploaded to your account.

## Drops

Drops send text or a file, up to 100 MB, from one signed-in browser to your
others. Open the drops button in the web app to send one or pick one up.

## On your Mac

If the Mac app is signed in to the same account, recordings you make in the
browser are copied into `~/Eavesy/Recordings` with their transcript and brief.
The audio stays in the cloud and is not downloaded.

## Browser and Mac compared

| | Browser | Mac app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Required | Optional |
| Records | Microphone, plus shared tab audio | Talk, Meeting, Video, Screenshot |
| Live captions | No | Yes, on-device |
| Files on disk | Copied to a signed-in Mac | Always |
