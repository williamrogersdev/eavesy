Most issues come down to a macOS permission or an audio-routing setting. Here are
the quick fixes.

## The recording has no sound

Eavesy captures the audio your Mac is **playing**, so:

1. Check **System Settings → Privacy &amp; Security → Screen &amp; System Audio
   Recording** and make sure Eavesy is allowed.
2. Confirm your call&rsquo;s audio is routed to the output device you can hear
   from — if it&rsquo;s going to a muted or different device, it won&rsquo;t be
   captured.
3. Make a short test recording, stop it, and inspect the processed transcript
   and audio before starting an important session.

## My voice isn&rsquo;t captured

Allow **Microphone** access in **System Settings → Privacy &amp; Security →
Microphone**, then start a fresh recording.

<Callout type="warning">
macOS only applies a newly granted permission to *new* recordings. If you
granted access mid-session, stop and start again.
</Callout>

## The app won&rsquo;t open on first launch

Because Eavesy is distributed outside the App Store, Gatekeeper may block the
first launch. Open **System Settings → Privacy &amp; Security**, scroll to the
bottom, and click **Open Anyway**. See
[Installing the Mac app](/docs/installing-the-mac-app).

## Processing is slow or the transcript is empty

- Check your internet connection — segments and completed browser captures need
  to upload before transcription can run.
- Long Mac recordings appear in processed batches rather than as sub-second
  captions.
- Very quiet input can produce an empty transcript; move closer to the mic or
  raise the other side&rsquo;s volume, then make a short test recording.

## My browser recording didn&rsquo;t finish uploading

Keep the tab open and choose **Try again** once you&rsquo;re back online; the
upload resumes where it stopped. You can also **Download** the recording so you
always have a copy. If the tab closed, the parts that already uploaded still
become a transcript within the hour.

## Still stuck?

Email <a href="mailto:support@eavesy.com">support@eavesy.com</a> — include your
macOS version and what you were recording, and we&rsquo;ll help.
