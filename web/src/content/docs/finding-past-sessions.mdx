Once you&rsquo;ve recorded a few sessions, the value compounds — every
conversation becomes searchable. Eavesy gives you two ways to find things.

## Browse and filter

Your dashboard lists every session with its title, date, and TL;DR. Filter by
date or scan the list to find a specific call.

## Semantic search

[Semantic search](/features/semantic-search) finds moments by **meaning**, not
just keywords. Ask *&ldquo;what did we decide about pricing?&rdquo;* and Eavesy
surfaces the exact part of the exact session where it came up — even if nobody
said the word &ldquo;pricing.&rdquo;

<Callout type="note" title="Where search runs">
Semantic search runs across the recordings and dictations saved to your account.
Local Markdown files are also searchable with any tool on your Mac. See
[Plans &amp; billing](/docs/plans-and-billing).
</Callout>

## Searching on disk

Because the Mac app writes every session as plain Markdown, your sessions are
also searchable by **any tool that reads files** — Spotlight, `grep`, your
editor, or an AI agent pointed at the folder. Nothing is locked in a proprietary
format. [The session folder format →](/docs/session-folder-format)
