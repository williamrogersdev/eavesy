Eavesy is built to stay out of your way — a floating panel you summon with a
hotkey, capture from, and dismiss, without switching apps.

## The global hotkey

The Mac app lives behind a **global hotkey** that opens the Eavesy panel from
anywhere, like a command palette. From the panel you can start a recording,
jump to a recent session, or run a quick capture.

<Callout type="note" title="Set and remap in Settings">
Eavesy&rsquo;s shortcuts are configurable. Open **Settings → Shortcuts** to see
the current bindings and remap any of them — including the global hotkey — to
keys that don&rsquo;t collide with your other tools.
</Callout>

## What you can do from the keyboard

- **Summon the panel** — open Eavesy over whatever you&rsquo;re doing.
- **Start / stop recording** — without leaving your call.
- **Quick capture** — grab audio, a screen recording, or a screenshot in one
  motion.
- **Search your sessions** — start typing to jump to a past session.

## In the browser

The web quick-capture bar uses a **leader key** followed by a letter to pick
what to capture (audio, video, or a screenshot). Open the bar to see the current
shortcuts for your browser.

## Standard macOS shortcuts

Inside a session, the usual macOS conventions apply:

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Copy the brief | `⌘ C` |
| Find in transcript | `⌘ F` |
| Close the panel | `Esc` |
