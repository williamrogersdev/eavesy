Eavesy is free to start with an account. A free account keeps your browser
recordings, transcripts, and briefs private and available when processing ends.

## Free

Everything you need to try Eavesy:

- Record in your browser
- No limit on recording length
- AI transcript + summary
- Export to Markdown &amp; audio

No payment or credit card is required to start.

## Pro — planned at $12/mo

For daily drivers who want the Mac app and the full library:

- Everything in Free
- **Mac app** — capture screen + system audio
- **Semantic search** and planned custom templates
- **Sync** across devices

<Callout type="note" title="Pro is coming soon">
Eavesy is launching free-first. Self-serve Pro billing is not open yet. The Mac
beta is available to [download today](/download/mac); the paid plan and its
remaining features will unlock when billing goes live. See the
[pricing page](/pricing) for the latest.
</Callout>

## Team — planned at $20/user/mo

For teams that need a shared workspace, SSO, and admin controls.
[Contact us](/contact) to set it up.

## Managing your billing

Existing paid accounts can open the Stripe billing portal from **Settings →
Plan &amp; billing**. New upgrades remain disabled while Pro is marked coming soon.
