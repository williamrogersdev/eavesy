The Mac app records calls without anything joining them. When you can't record
from your Mac (you're on another computer, on your phone, or not in the call at
all), you can send the **Eavesy Notetaker** instead. It joins the call as a
participant and records it for you.

It works with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Zoom isn't supported yet.

<Callout type="note">
Everyone in the call sees "Eavesy Notetaker" join, and it posts in the chat that
it's transcribing. Tell people it records, and follow your organization's
policy and the consent rules where you and they are.
</Callout>

## Send it

1. In the web app, open **Notetaker**.
2. Paste the meeting link, from `meet.google.com` or a Teams invite.
3. Leave **Start time** empty to send it now, or pick a time up to 60 days ahead.
4. If the meeting has a waiting room or lobby, admit **Eavesy Notetaker** when it
   asks to join.

You can have up to 10 notetakers scheduled or in calls at once. Cancel one
before it joins, or choose **Make it leave** to end its recording early.

## During and after the call

The transcript fills in while the call goes on, a minute or so behind. Open it
from the notetaker's row or from your library.

Lines are labelled with the name of whoever the call shows speaking. When the
call doesn't show who is speaking, lines aren't attributed to anyone.

When the call ends, the notetaker leaves and a brief is written on top of the
transcript, the same as for a recording from your Mac.

## When it leaves

The notetaker leaves on its own when:

- the meeting ends, or it's removed from the call,
- everyone else has left,
- nobody else joined within 15 minutes,
- nobody admitted it from the waiting room within 15 minutes, or
- it has been in the call for 4 hours.

## When it can't join

- **The meeting only admits signed-in accounts or people in the organization.**
  The notetaker joins as a guest, so meetings that turn guests away can't be
  recorded this way. Ask the organizer to allow guests, or record from your Mac.
- **Nobody admitted it.** Someone in the call has to let it in from the waiting
  room or lobby.
- **The link isn't a meeting.** Paste the full link from the invite.

The notetaker's row says why it didn't record.

## What it records

Only the call's audio, plus the participants' display names and who was speaking
when. It doesn't record video or the screen. The audio, transcript and brief are
stored and deleted like your other recordings.
