Eavesy is **context capture for AI agents**. It records your screen plus the
audio your Mac is already playing and your microphone, processes the recording
into a transcript and structured Markdown brief you can paste into any AI
agent, then saves everything as a real folder on your disk.

No bot joins your call or appears in the participant list. You still need to
tell people when required. The Mac app keeps local source files and sends a
private cloud copy for transcription, summaries, search, sync, and recovery.

## The two ways to use it

- **The browser recorder** — create a free account and record without a
  download. It captures your microphone or a shared screen and uploads as
  you record for a transcript and AI brief, with no length limit. [Learn more →](/docs/browser-recorder)
- **The Mac app** — captures your **screen and system audio** for full-fidelity
  capture, unlimited length, sync, semantic search, and files written straight
  to your disk. [Install it →](/docs/installing-the-mac-app)

<Callout type="tip">
Not sure which to start with? Try the browser recorder to get a feel for it,
then install the Mac app when you want to capture full calls with system audio.
</Callout>

## The fastest path

1. Open the [browser recorder](/sign-up) or
   [download the Mac app](/download/mac).
2. Press **record** and start your call, walkthrough, or conversation.
3. Watch the recording status and elapsed time while you talk.
4. Press **stop** and wait for processing — then copy the transcript and brief
   into your agent.

## What you get from every session

- A clean **title** and **TL;DR**
- **Decisions** and owner-tagged **action items**
- The complete, copyable **transcript**
- The **audio**, and a **Markdown + JSON** copy you own
