Eavesy is a **local, AI-first assistant**. You drop things into it (text or
an image from your clipboard, screenshots, dictation, and recordings of your
screen and voice) and it turns them into clean, structured Markdown context that
is quick to paste into any AI agent or reach over MCP. Everything is saved as
files on your disk.

Recording is one capability, not the whole product. When you record, no bot
joins your call or appears in the participant list. You still need to tell
people when required. The Mac app keeps local source files and sends audio to a
private cloud copy for transcription, summaries, search, sync, and recovery.

## The two ways to use it

- **The browser recorder** — create a free account and record without a
  download. It captures your microphone or a shared screen and uploads as
  you record for a transcript and AI brief, with no length limit. [Learn more →](/docs/browser-recorder)
- **The Mac app** — captures your **screen and system audio** for full-fidelity
  capture, unlimited length, sync, semantic search, and files written straight
  to your disk. [Install it →](/docs/installing-the-mac-app)

<Callout type="tip">
Not sure which to start with? Try the browser recorder to get a feel for it,
then install the Mac app when you want to capture full calls with system audio.
</Callout>

## The fastest path

1. [Download the Mac app](/download/mac) (or open the
   [browser recorder](/sign-up)).
2. Open the launcher with your global hotkey. Press **⌘V** to save text or an
   image from your clipboard, take a screenshot, dictate, or start a recording.
3. For a recording, press **stop** and wait for processing.
4. Copy the Markdown into your agent, or
   [connect it over MCP](/docs/connect-mcp).

## What you get from a recording

- A clean **title** and **TL;DR**
- **Decisions** and owner-tagged **action items**
- The complete, copyable **transcript**
- The **audio**, and a **Markdown + JSON** copy you own
