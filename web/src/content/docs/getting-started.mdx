Eavesy is a small Mac app that keeps what you say, hear, see and copy as plain
files on your disk. Each capture becomes Markdown you can paste into an AI
agent, open in any editor, or let an agent read over MCP.

Eavesy is free and [open source](/docs/open-source). The Mac app works without
an account. Signing in adds cloud transcripts, briefs, search and sync.

## Ways to capture

| Mode | What it records | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Talk](/docs/talk-and-dictation) | Your microphone | Dictation and voice memos |
| [Meeting](/docs/meetings) | Your microphone and the audio your Mac plays | Calls and in-person conversations |
| [Video](/docs/video) | Your screen, its audio and your microphone | Walkthroughs and bug reports |
| [Screenshot](/docs/screenshots-and-notes) | An area of your screen | Something you want an agent to see |
| [Clipboard and notes](/docs/screenshots-and-notes) | Text or an image you copied, or a file you drop in | Messages, snippets, reference files |

Recording from your Mac adds no bot to your calls. For a Meet or Teams call you
can't record from your Mac, you can send the [Notetaker](/docs/notetaker), which
joins as a visible participant. Either way, you are responsible for telling
people when you record them.

## Your first capture

1. [Install the Mac app](/docs/installing-the-mac-app). Download the DMG or
   run `brew install --cask williamrogersdev/eavesy/eavesy`.
2. Open Eavesy with **⌘⇧Space**. Grant the Microphone permission when asked.
3. Press **⌘⇧D**, say a sentence, and press **⌘⇧D** again. If your cursor was
   in a text field, the words are pasted there.
4. Open the launcher again to see the capture. It is also saved as a folder in
   `~/Eavesy/Recordings`.

From here you can [record a meeting](/docs/meetings), copy the Markdown into
an agent, or [connect an agent over MCP](/docs/connect-mcp).

## With or without an account

Without an account, everything stays on your Mac. Talk gives you on-device
text, and meetings get on-device live captions while they run.

When you sign in at eavesy.com, the Mac app also uploads audio so you get:

- a full cloud transcript that replaces the live captions,
- a brief with a title, summary, decisions and action items,
- search and chat across your recordings, and
- access for agents over [MCP](/docs/connect-mcp).

You can also [record in the browser](/docs/browser-recorder) without
installing anything. Browser recordings need an account.
