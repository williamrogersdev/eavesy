Video mode records your screen, the audio your Mac plays, and your microphone.
Use it to walk through a bug, show a design, or explain a flow to an agent.

## Record a video

1. Open the launcher with **⌘⇧Space** and choose **Video**.
2. Do what you want to show, and talk through it.
3. Choose stop when you're done.

Video needs the Screen Recording permission as well as the Microphone
permission.

## What gets saved

Each video is saved as a folder named like `2026-06-15 Screen recording`,
holding `screen.mp4`, `audio.m4a`, and the usual Markdown files. When you're
signed in, the audio is transcribed and gets a brief, the same as a
[meeting](/docs/meetings).

See [your files](/docs/session-folder-format) for the full layout.
