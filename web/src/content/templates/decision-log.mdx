A decision log preserves what was agreed and why, so a later reader can distinguish the choice from the conversation around it. Use one entry per decision. The template is plain Markdown and works independently of Eavesy.

## Distinguish proposals from agreements

Set the status explicitly. A speaker saying “we could ship Friday” has proposed a date; “we agreed to ship Friday” establishes a different kind of claim. If the discussion never resolved the choice, use needs confirmation rather than writing a final decision.

## Keep a source pointer

Add the recording title, a timestamp when available, and a short supporting excerpt. A summary is convenient for navigation, but the underlying transcript and audio help you check nuance. Retain enough surrounding context to understand whether a later sentence reversed the decision.

## Leave missing fields empty

Do not assign the speaker as owner merely because they described the decision. If no due date was stated, write “not assigned” rather than generating one. Separate a requested next action from something the participants actually committed to doing.

## Worked example

Decision: launch on Friday. Status: agreed. Supporting excerpt: “We agreed to launch on Friday.” Owner: not established. This is a deliberately small illustrative source, also used in the [cited answer example](/examples/library-answer-with-sources). It does not establish a specific calendar date or time zone.

## Use it with Eavesy

Find the relevant recording, check the passage, and copy the decision and source into this note. Save it beside the recording or in your notes vault. If a later meeting changes the plan, add a new entry and mark the old one superseded instead of silently replacing the historical record.


## Copyable template

```markdown
# Decision log

## Decision
[One sentence stating the agreed choice.]

## Status
[Proposed / agreed / superseded / needs confirmation]

## Date and scope
- Decision date:
- Project or topic:

## Reasoning
[Why was this choice made? Use only established reasons.]

## Source
- Recording or document:
- Timestamp or section:
- Supporting excerpt:

## Ownership and follow-up
- Owner, if assigned:
- Next step:
- Due date, if agreed:

## Open questions
[What remains unestablished?]

## Superseded by
[Link to a later decision when applicable.]
```
