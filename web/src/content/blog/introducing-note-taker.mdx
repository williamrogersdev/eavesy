Your AI agent is only as good as the context you give it — and most of that
context lives in things it never saw: the call you just had, the screen you were
walking through, the thing you said out loud. Getting it into the agent means
retyping, screenshotting, and re-explaining. We built **Eavesy** to skip all of
that: hit record, and it turns what you see and say into a brief you can paste
straight into Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT.

## Eyes and ears

Eavesy captures your screen plus the audio your Mac is already playing and your
microphone. That means any call — Zoom, Google Meet, Teams — a screen
walkthrough, or an in-person conversation, with no bot in the participant list
and no bot joining the participant list.

## A brief, not just a transcript

When you stop, Eavesy distills the session into a clean Markdown brief: a TL;DR,
the key decisions, the steps you took, and the full transcript underneath. It's
structured to paste straight into an AI agent — one copy, and your agent is
caught up.

## Context you own

Every session is written to a real folder on your disk: Markdown, JSON, and
audio. Point an agent at it, open it in Obsidian, grep it from the terminal. If
you ever stop using Eavesy, your context stays exactly where it is.
