The first public release of Eavesy.

- **Mac app** for Apple silicon. Record calls and in-person conversations from
  your Mac, with no bot on the call.
- **Briefs** with a title, summary, decisions and action items, written on top
  of the full transcript and ready to paste into any agent.
- **Files you own.** Every recording is saved to your disk as Markdown, JSON
  and audio.
- **Browser recorder** for recording from eavesy.com with a free account.
- **Search** across your recordings by meaning.

Brief templates, live captions and more have shipped since. See the
[docs](/docs) for what Eavesy does today.
