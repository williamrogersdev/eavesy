The first public release of Eavesy is here.

- **Screen + voice capture** — record your screen, system audio, and mic on
  macOS, with no bot on the call and support for in-person sessions.
- **AI-ready brief** — rolling segment transcription plus a structured Markdown
  brief you can paste into Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT after processing.
- **Context you own** — every session written to your disk as Markdown, JSON, and
  audio.
- **Browser recorder** — create a free account and record a short session without installing the Mac app.
- **Semantic search** across completed sessions.

Custom output templates remain on the roadmap and are not part of this release.
